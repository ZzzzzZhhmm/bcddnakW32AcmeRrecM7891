\paragraph{Recorded-prefix runtime measurement.}
We measured the complete policy replanning path on one NVIDIA H100 80\,GB
using BF16, batch size one, 10 denoising evaluations, $H=32$, and $K=32$.
The workload comprised 200 replans from five held-out Press Button
demonstrations, with 40 predeclared prefixes per episode and 20 additional
warm-up replans excluded from measurement.
Median and 95th-percentile latency were 500.0 and 553.8\,ms, respectively;
peak PyTorch-allocated GPU memory was 23.62\,GiB.
CUDA-synchronized timing included preprocessing, visual encoding,
retrieval, history reading, policy inference, action conversion, and
standard telemetry, but excluded model loading, disk decoding, and simulation.
The repertoire contained 89,680 events.
This measurement used the 300-step development checkpoint, whose natural
source gate was zero at all measured prefixes.
Recorded observations and action histories were replayed without executing
the generated commands; these measurements therefore characterize this
workload, not closed-loop success, the mature policy's active-source runtime,
or a speedup over another method.
